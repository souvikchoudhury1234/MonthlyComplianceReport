                      The 1:1 (30 min · within 5 business days)
                      1. Open (2 min): "I want to talk through your September QC — this isn't disciplinary, it's coaching."
                      2. Listen to 2 flagged calls together (15 min): pick the lowest-QC call plus one representative call. Play, pause at the failure moment, ask "what were you thinking here?" before offering a fix.
                      3. Name the specific pattern (5 min): point to the exact behavior (see box below), not the QC score.
                      4. Written action plan (5 min): two specific behaviors the agent will change. Agent writes it, lead witnesses.
                      5. Set the check-in (3 min): 2-week follow-up on calendar before the meeting ends.
                      The two behaviors to fix (highest-ROI)
                      1. Active listening — before you ask your next question, ask yourself: did the payer just answer this, or something adjacent?
                      252 of 253 September escalations flagged repeated questioning. 198 flagged failure to adapt. This is one behavior, five labels.
                      2. First 30 seconds — agent name, program name, recording disclosure. In that order. No skipping when the payer answers early.
                      22 of 40 September compliance failures were "program name / intro missing." Muscle-memory issue, not knowledge.
                      What "adapting" sounds like on a call
                      • Payer volunteers deductible mid-call: skip your deductible question. Acknowledge ("noted, $500 deductible") and move on.
                      • Payer says "we don't cover that at all": don't ask copay/coinsurance for that service. Pivot to alternatives.
                      • Payer sounds impatient: shorten remaining preamble. Batch related asks into one question.
                      • Payer answers question 2 while answering question 1: mentally cross question 2 off. Ask the next unanswered item.
                      Practice drill (weekly, 15 min)
                      1. Lead reads a scripted payer response containing 3 pieces of info
                      2. Agent identifies which script questions they can now skip
                      3. Repeat with 5 different payer responses
                      4. Debrief on what got missed
                      Follow-up rules
                      • 2-week check-in: 15-min touch on calendar, listen to 1 new call together
                      • Next monthly QC report: if agent is still red-flagged, escalate to Ravi Thakur / Indranil Dey
                      • If improving: keep the check-in cadence, celebrate the win publicly in team huddle
                      What to avoid
                      • Reading QC scores aloud — talk about specific moments, not numbers
                      • Coaching multiple behaviors at once — pick two, drop the rest
                      • Using the coaching session for policy news, roster changes, or other business
                      • Skipping the written action plan — verbal commitments don't stick
